Common questions

Giving FAQ

Is my donation tax deductible?

Yes. NGIM is a registered 501(c)3 nonprofit, and all donations are tax deductible in the United States. You'll receive a receipt for your records.

Can I give by check or other means?

Yes — checks, donor-advised funds, and stock gifts are all welcome. Contact us and we'll walk you through it.

How do I change or cancel a monthly gift?

Anytime, no questions asked — manage your gift from the link in your receipt or contact us directly.

Can I designate my gift to a specific project?

Yes. During checkout you can direct your gift toward international training, the film and study guides, or where most needed.
